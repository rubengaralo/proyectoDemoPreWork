# All-Hands Meeting — Level 10 Framework

**Day:** Monday (weekly)
**Time:** 10:00 – 11:30 AM (America/Santo_Domingo)
**Duration:** 90 minutes — same day, same time, same agenda, every week
**First session:** Monday, August 24, 2026
**Reference video:** https://youtu.be/sepg2j0NTXc?si=Xm1IXRGrX3XKmGPr

## Why Monday (not Friday)

The Level 10 Meeting (from EOS — the Entrepreneurial Operating System, *Traction* by
Gino Wickman) works because issues get solved and converted into to-dos the team
executes immediately. Holding it Monday means:

- Decisions and to-dos drive the entire week ahead, instead of sitting idle over the weekend.
- The scorecard reviews last week's numbers while they are still fresh.
- Friday afternoons already carry end-of-week fatigue and the ED's weekly review block,
  so a Friday all-hands would compete with wrap-up work.

## Agenda (90 minutes)

| # | Segment | Time | What happens |
|---|---------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Segue / Good news | 5 min | Each person shares one personal and one professional highlight. Connects the team before the work. |
| 2 | Scorecard | 5 min | Review the 5–15 weekly numbers. Each metric is simply "on track" or "off track" — off-track items drop to the Issues List. No discussion here. |
| 3 | Rock review | 5 min | Each quarterly Rock (priority) is "on track" or "off track". Off-track Rocks drop to the Issues List. No discussion here. |
| 4 | Headlines | 5 min | Short customer/donor/employee news — good or bad. Anything needing discussion drops to the Issues List. |
| 5 | To-do list | 5 min | Review last week's to-dos. Each is "done" or "not done". Target: 90%+ done. Not-done items stay or become issues. |
| 6 | IDS (Identify, Discuss, Solve) | 60 min | The heart of the meeting. Pick the 3 most important issues, then for each: **Identify** the real root cause, **Discuss** it once (no politicking, no tangents), **Solve** it with a concrete to-do and owner. Repeat until time runs out. |
| 7 | Conclude | 5 min | Recap new to-dos and owners, agree on cascading messages (who needs to be told what), and each person rates the meeting 1–10. Average below 8 means the meeting itself becomes an issue. |

## Ground rules

- **Starts on time, ends on time.** Even if people are missing.
- **Same day, same time, same agenda, every week.** No cancellations — reschedule only for holidays, and to the same week.
- One facilitator keeps the agenda moving; one person keeps the Issues List and To-Do List visible to everyone.
- Discussion only happens inside IDS. Everything else is reporting — anything worth discussing "drops down" to the Issues List.
- To-dos are 7-day commitments with a single owner.
- Score the meeting honestly every week; the rating is what keeps it a "Level 10" meeting instead of drifting back to a status meeting.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Facilitator | Keeps time and moves the agenda forward |
| Scribe | Maintains the Scorecard, Issues List, and To-Do List |
| All | Arrive prepared: numbers updated, to-dos done, issues added before the meeting |
